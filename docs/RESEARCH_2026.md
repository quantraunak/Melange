# What worked, what faded: consumer social and in-person, 2023–2026

October 2026. Four research passes, each with sources for every number:

- [`research/R_winners.md`](research/R_winners.md) — 18 apps that grew or lasted
- [`research/R_failures.md`](research/R_failures.md) — 17 that went viral and faded
- [`research/R_irl.md`](research/R_irl.md) — in-person, friendship and events businesses
- [`research/R_future.md`](research/R_future.md) — where 18–30-year-olds' social lives are heading, and who is buying

Many figures come from third-party estimators or company claims; each report
marks how far to trust them. Use the numbers as direction, not precision.

## What kept working

| Pattern | Evidence |
|---|---|
| The invite works before the download | Partiful: guests RSVP without the app and see who's going; monthly users up ~400% |
| One closed network at a time | Fizz campus by campus; Timeleft needs 50+ people a week per city |
| Content of you and your friends, kept private | Locket: 9M daily users, half of monthly users open daily, profitable |
| A recurring real-world occasion | Timeleft's Wednesday dinners (~€18M/yr reported); Strava run clubs up 3.5× in 2025 |
| Useful before your friends arrive | Beli, Letterboxd, Strava each work as a personal log first |
| Charge for the seat, keep the social part free | Paid formats earn; free friendship apps earn close to nothing |

## What made apps fade

| Failure | Examples |
|---|---|
| A novelty with no reason to come back | Poparazzi (~4M → ~2K monthly), Lapse, BeReal |
| One feature an incumbent could copy | Clubhouse, BeReal |
| Forced invites or fake engagement | Lapse (−70%), NGL (FTC), IRL (95% bots) |
| Safety and privacy blowups | NGL, Tea, Hive |
| Spikes from outside events mistaken for demand | Clubhouse, Lemon8, Bluesky |

## Where things are heading

- Paid swiping is shrinking (Bumble payers −16% in a year; sold at about a tenth of its IPO value) while in-person formats grow.
- Loneliness peaks at 18–29 (~30% vs 21% of all adults, Gallup 2025).
- Friends moved from feeds to group chats (friends' content fell from 11% to 7% of Instagram time).
- As feeds fill with AI content, proof of real people and real meetups becomes a feature.
- Active buyers of consumer social: Match Group, Ithaca (Bumble), Strava, Voodoo.

## What Melange took from it

1. Nights are the product; the app is where they continue.
2. RSVP from the web, no download needed.
3. A night's photos are private to the people who were there.
4. Dense in one scene (LA) before opening more.
5. Measure attendance and return visits, not signups.
