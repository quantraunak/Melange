# Consumer social / taste / photo / friend apps, 2023-2026: who won, why, with numbers

Compiled 2026-10-20 from web search plus direct fetches of TechCrunch, CNBC/NBC, Semafor, Match Group IR and SEC filings.
Confidence tags: **[V]** = primary or first-tier press (company filing or company statement in TechCrunch/CNBC/WSJ). **[3P]** = third-party estimate (Sensor Tower, Appfigures, Similarweb). **[U]** = unverified or secondary-only (aggregators, AI-generated Sacra pages, blogs). Third-party MAU numbers are estimates, not counts.

## 1. App-by-app

### Partiful (event invites)
- **Inflection:** founded 2020. iOS app 2023, Android 2024. Became Gen Z default in 2024. Google Play Best App 2024. Got free press in Feb 2025 when Apple launched Invites and Partiful joked about "copycats."
- **Numbers:**
  - Q1 2025 average MAU ~500K, up 400% YoY [3P Sensor Tower, via CNBC 2025-04-19](https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/business/money-report/meet-partiful-the-gen-z-party-planning-staple-thats-taking-on-apple/3820670/).
  - Added 2M new users Jan to Apr 2025 [V, CEO].
  - "60%+ of active users check weekly" [V, company].
  - 9 in 10 users are in the US.
  - ~$20M Series A led by a16z (2022). ~$27M total raised and a "$140M valuation" appear only in [U Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/partiful).
  - Team of 25.
- **Loop:** a host creates an event and texts a link. Guests RSVP on the web without installing, and see who else is going (the guest list is the social proof). Afterwards come photo albums, text blasts and "boops." Every party exposes 10-50 non-users to the brand, and some of them become hosts. Each new host brings their own guest list. A free host is the acquisition engine.
- **Atomic network:** one friend group or one party. Densest among post-grads moving to new cities (Murthy). a16z made it mandatory for NY Tech Week 2025, which pulled it upmarket into pro events [U Sacra].
- **Retention:** Weekly checking is the claimed signal. Users say a gathering without a Partiful "feels lost."
- **Monetization:** almost none. Group Order (Instacart party supplies) is the only revenue feature.

### Beli (restaurant ranking)
- **Inflection:** App Store launch June 2021 in NYC. Exploded with Gen Z in 2024-25.
- **Numbers (review counts are the only public scale proxy; DAU withheld):**
  - Reviews per year: 13M (2023), 30M (2024), 25M+ YTD 2025. Yelp's comparable figures are 22M (2023) and 21M (2024) [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/fn-dish/news/beli-app-trend).
  - Cumulative ratings: 58M+ (WSJ, May 2025). 75M+ across 30K cities (Sep 2025, Wikipedia). 120M+ across 2M restaurants and 50K cities ([Boston Globe, 2026-08-04](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/08/04/lifestyle/beli-restaurant-rating-app/)).
  - 80% of users are under 35. About 80% of growth comes from referrals or word of mouth (founders) [U Substack](https://startupsignals.substack.com/p/beli-your-food-diary-and-dining-advisor).
  - $5.3M round (Goodwater) Nov 2023. ~$12M total raised by June 2025 (FirstMark).
- **Loop:** you never type a star rating. You answer "was this better or worse than X?" and Beli turns that into a 1-10 score. You see only friends' ratings. Your list and ranking become an identity object (number of restaurants ranked, city leaderboards), and you share your list and recruit friends so your feed gets better. The core is pairwise ranking plus a friend-only graph plus leaderboards.
- **Atomic network:** friend group in one city (NYC first, then Chicago and SF).
- **Retention:** ratings grow faster than Yelp's. It gets better the more you log, since your personal ranking deepens.
- **Monetization:** minimal. A NYC supper-club subscription is being tested. Monetization is still unsolved.

### Locket Widget (photo to friends' home screens)
- **Inflection:** viral in Jan 2022 (2M users in two weeks). Durable since then.
- **Numbers:**
  - 9M+ DAU, 80M+ downloads, 10B+ photos, profitable since 2024 on $12.5M raised ([TechCrunch 2025-08-06](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/06/photo-sharing-app-locket-is-banking-on-a-new-celebrity-focused-feature-to-fuel-its-growth)) [V company].
  - 91M+ lifetime installs [3P Appfigures]. 100K+ subscribers, 15 employees ([TechCrunch 2025-11-03](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/03/lockets-social-app-is-picking-up-steam-with-gen-alpha)).
  - Rollcall: 25%+ of actives post weekly, 1M shares in week one, ~80% of early Rollcall users are Gen Alpha.
  - 2026 press release: DAU up 22% YTD, DAU/MAU 51% [V self-reported, PR].
  - Ad revenue +87% after Moloco SDK [U vendor case study].
- **Loop:** a photo lands on up to 20 close friends' home screens. The recipient is pulled to reply with a photo of their own. Install requires a friend, because the widget is empty without one.
- **Atomic network:** 2-20 close friends or a partner (it started as a gift to the founder's girlfriend).
- **Extensions:** Celebrity Lockets (artists get 1K-15K fan slots; 17% of Suki Waterhouse's 5K fans were new to Locket) and Rollcall (Live Activity weekly recap).
- **Monetization:** Locket Gold subscription plus ads.

### Retro (weekly photo journal for friends)
- **Inflection:** launched 2023 by ex-Instagram engineers Nathan Sharp (who led Stories) and Ryan Olson. Broke out in Asia, then Canada and Europe. In 2025 it was #1 photo app in 12 countries and #1 overall in 6 ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91462432/retro-photo-sharing-app)).
- **Numbers:**
  - ~7M downloads [3P Appfigures].
  - In-app spend up 460% in 180 days ([TechCrunch 2026-08-28](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/friend-focused-photo-sharing-app-retro-snags-21m/)).
  - 7.8M downloads and 1.7M MAU [3P Sensor Tower via Dealroom].
  - $21M+ Series A (Thrive and Field Ventures), closed Dec 2025. Valuation >$100M [3P PitchBook].
  - ~$36/yr subscription, introduced 2025. No ads.
- **Loop:** each week you post that week's photos (camera roll, low effort) and see only friends who also posted. Recaps are shareable. Sharp's stated design goal is participation rate, fighting the power law where a few people post for everyone.
- **Atomic network:** close friends and family.

### Yope (private friend photo network), the 2025-26 breakout
- **Numbers:**
  - Launched Sep 2024 (formerly Salo). 2.2M MAU and 800K DAU within ~6 months [U TechLeap].
  - $4.65M seed at $50M valuation (Goodwater, Feb 2025).
  - $12.3M pre-A led by Northzone ([TechCrunch 2026-07-22](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/yope-raises-12-3m-to-build-a-private-social-network-without-algorithms-or-ads/)).
  - ~15M registered users. 50%+ of users open it 5+ days a week. 10-20M content items a week. ~20% of actives invited an older family member.
- **Loop:** contact sync builds small private groups. The collage "wall" and AI recaps, plus lock-screen widgets, bring people back. The insight came from 100K interviews: 30% of young people already ran "photo dump" or "spam" accounts.
- **Monetization:** planned subscription. No ads.

### Airbuds (music widget social)
- 15M+ downloads, 5M MAU, 1.5M DAU [V company via TechCrunch].
- ~$10M raised: 776 led the $5M round, plus a16z. Nikita Bier is an angel investor.
- **Loop:** your friends' live listening shows up on a widget, and you react.
- **Atomic network:** friend group.

### Saturn (high-school calendar social)
- **Numbers:**
  - Reportedly at ~80% of US high schools [V TechCrunch].
  - $44M+ raised (2021).
  - Acquired by Snap in June 2025, terms undisclosed, ~30 staff ([TechCrunch 2025-06-20](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/20/snap-acquires-saturn-a-social-calendar-app-for-high-school-and-college-students)).
- **Loop:** your class schedule (block or rotation) is useless until classmates join. The app shows who shares your lunch period or class, so each student pulls in their schoolmates. It is utility first, then a social graph.
- **Atomic network:** one high school.
- **What went wrong:** no standalone monetization path. Parents raised safety complaints (fake profiles could see schedules). It exited to Snap as an acqui-hire-ish deal rather than an independent company.

### Fizz (anonymous campus feed)
- **Numbers:**
  - Stanford, 2021. Seed $4.5M, A $12M (Nov 2022), B $25M (2023, Owl/NEA). $41M raised.
  - 620+ US campuses as of Sep 2025, 250+ launched in one month. DAU 4x in a month. One-day registration record broken 12 days running. 27M posts ([TechCrunch 2025-09-03](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/03/college-social-app-fizz-expands-into-grocery-delivery)).
  - Earlier: 3.5M MAU on 300+ campuses [U].
  - Saudi Arabia launch 2026: #1 in 48h [U Global Dating Insights].
  - Strategic round at a $200M cap (2026) [U, BI via search snippet; not fetched].
- **Loop:** you verify with a .edu address, and everyone on campus is in one feed. The content is about your school and your people, and the launch pattern is campus by campus.
- **Atomic network:** one campus.
- **Monetization:** marketplace, Gopuff grocery delivery and ads. Not proven.
- **Risks:** anonymity brings moderation and safety load. The Sidechat rivalry is noted in [U] sources.

### Lapse (disposable-camera friends app). Spiked then faded
- **Numbers:**
  - #1 US App Store in Sep-Oct 2023. Peak 210-218K downloads a day, then down 70% to 63K by Nov 2023 ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20231117/5-lapse-loses-momentum-as-downloads-drop-70-percent)).
  - $30M Series A at ~$150M valuation (Greylock/DST, Feb 2024). ~100M photos a month claimed [V company via TechCrunch].
  - No 2025-26 growth news found.
- **What went wrong:** growth came from forced invites (you had to invite friends to use it) rather than pull. That produced a chart spike, not retention. The product later pivoted to a "photo journal," because that was what power users did with it.

### Letterboxd (film diary and taste)
- **Numbers:**
  - Members: 1.8M (2020), 26M+ (filings, Apr 2026), 30M+ (Jul 2026). +10M in Jan 2025-Jan 2026 alone ([Semafor 2026-04-26](https://www.semafor.com/article/04/26/2026/whats-next-for-letterboxd); Wikipedia).
  - 2025 activity: 898.5M films logged (+28%), 143.6M reviews (+49%), 12.9M lists (+88%) [V-ish, Letterboxd Year in Review via Wikipedia].
  - Tiny bought 60% at a $50-60M valuation (2023). Sale process in 2026 floats ~$250M, with Netflix, Sony and Paramount reported interested [U press reports].
  - Most users are 18-34.
- **Loop:** you log a film, rate it and write a short witty review. Reviews get screenshotted to TikTok and X, and outsiders join to find out who is behind the review. The four-favorites profile works as a taste ID card (that framing is my inference).
- **Atomic network:** taste peers (asymmetric follows). Celebrity users such as Charli XCX add signal.
- **Monetization:** Pro and Patron subscriptions plus film-marketing ads. A durable, slow compounder: 15x in 6 years.

### Strava (incl. run clubs)
- **Numbers:**
  - 180M+ registered users. ~50M MAU in 2025 [3P Sensor Tower via TechCrunch 2025-10-12](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/12/strava-eyes-ipo-as-gen-z-trades-dating-apps-for-running-clubs/). 14B kudos in 2025.
  - New clubs nearly 4x in 2025, to 1M total. Running clubs 3.5x. 2024 run-club membership +59%.
  - Gen Z is the fastest-growing cohort: Gen Z half-marathon participation +31%, marathons +33%.
  - $2.2B valuation (Sequoia-led, May 2025). CEO says revenue is heading toward ~$500M [V CEO]. Confidential IPO filing reported Jan 2026 [U-ish].
  - Premium penetration ~2% of registered users [U Sacra].
- **Loop:** you post an activity (the content is your own body and effort) and get kudos from friends. Clubs and group runs then turn the online graph into an in-person one, and "Strava or it didn't happen" drives logging.
- **Atomic network:** local club or running group. Run clubs are explicitly replacing dating apps (1 in 5 respondents in the 2024 report had dated someone met at a run club [survey]).
- **Monetization:** subscription (~90% of revenue in 2023 per [U Sacra]).

### Substack (social side)
- **Numbers:**
  - 4M paid subscriptions (Nov 2024), 5M (Mar 2025). $100M at a $1.1B valuation (Jul 2025, Bond/Chernin) [V].
  - App drives 30%+ of paid subscriptions [U Sacra].
  - Notes DAU ~1M [U]. Growth may be decelerating (Simon Owens: still not at 6M) [U].
- **Loop:** a writer's audience is portable. Notes and recommendations cross-pollinate subscribers between writers, so every recommendation acts as a referral. The 2025 pivot made the app a social feed rather than an inbox.
- **Monetization:** 10% of paid subscriptions.

### Pinterest (Gen Z resurgence)
- **Numbers:**
  - MAU 578M in Q2 2025 (+11%), 631M in Q1 2026 (record, +11%) [V earnings].
  - Gen Z is more than 50% of users and the fastest-growing cohort [V CEO]. eMarketer says ~42% [3P conflict].
- **Why:** it is "the un-social social network": no follower pressure, curation as self-expression (boards are taste identity), plus AI visual search and shopping. Gen Z use it to plan a self, not to perform one.
- **Monetization:** ads; revenue above $1B a quarter.

### Hinge vs Tinder
- **Hinge:**
  - FY2025 direct revenue +$140.4M (+26%), roughly $680M (my arithmetic). Payers +18%, RPP +7% ([Match 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/891103/000089110326000025/mtch-20251231.htm)).
  - Q4 2025: $186M (+26%), 1.9M payers, RPP $32.96 [V-ish, MarketBeat call summary]. MAU in European expansion markets up nearly 50%.
- **Tinder:** direct revenue -4% through 9M 2025. Payers -7%. MAU -9% YoY (Q1 2025) [V 10-Q].
- **Why Hinge wins:** prompts and profiles show taste and personality instead of a swipe-on-face. It is positioned for real-world outcomes, and its payers pay ~2x Tinder RPP ($33 vs $16).
- **What went wrong at Tinder:** swipe fatigue, a game-like loop with no outcome, and Gen Z moving to in-person settings (run clubs, Partiful parties).

### Lemon8 (ByteDance). Faded
- 2023 push via TikTok creators. US DAU on Similarweb nearly halved Apr-May 2023 [3P Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2023/lemon8-app-bytedance-user-decline/) (the absolute figures are web-panel numbers, so treat with care).
- Brief spike in Dec 2024 (12.5M global MAU) as a TikTok-ban refuge [3P Sensor Tower]. Pulled from stores Jan 18, 2025.
- The US is now under the TikTok USDS JV [U].
- **What went wrong:** growth was bought through paid creators and cross-promotion, with no native loop or atomic network. The feed was weaker than TikTok's.

### Threads
- **Numbers:**
  - Launched Jul 2023 and bootstrapped from the Instagram graph.
  - MAU: 150M (Apr 2024), 275M (Q3 2024), 320M (early 2025), 400M (Aug 2025), 500M (Jun 2026) [V for the earlier figures; 500M is U, secondary only].
  - 150M+ DAU (Q3 2025 call) [V]. Ads rolled out globally.
- **Why it worked:** a borrowed graph (one-tap Instagram import) plus heavy cross-promotion inside Instagram, plus an algorithmic feed tuned for time spent (+10% time spent from ranking alone in Q3 2025).
- **Lesson:** it does not apply to startups, since it depends on distribution you do not have. The early drop (DAU fell ~5x after launch week) shows the graph got users in but did not keep them. The ranking work did that later.

### Bluesky. Spiked then declined
- 46M registered (Aug 2026), but mobile MAU fell from a 22.1M average in Q4 2024 to 10.7M in Q2 2026, down 52%. DAU ~3M (-25.6% YoY) [3P Similarweb via TechCrunch, Aug 2026](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/08/16/0326249/). DAU/MAU ~29%.
- **What went wrong:** growth was driven by events (refugees from X after news moments), not by a loop. When the reason to leave X fades, there is no daily reason to stay.

### Sora app (OpenAI, cameos). Launched and killed
- Launched Sep 30, 2025. Downloads went 0.5M (Sep), 3.2-3.3M peak (Nov), 2.2M (Dec), 1.2M (Jan 2026), ~1.1M (Feb). $1.4-2.1M lifetime IAP. Fell out of the US top 100. Shutdown announced Mar 24, 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/openais-sora-was-the-creepiest-app-on-your-phone-now-its-shutting-down/)).
- **Cameo loop:** scan your face, and friends can cast you in videos. The content is literally "you," and invites carry your likeness.
- **What went wrong:** novelty without a recurring reason. Generation was expensive, and there was deepfake backlash. A lawsuit by Cameo forced a rename to "characters." There was no atomic network (public cameos, not friend groups) and no utility.

### Other 2025-26 signals
- **Tea (women's dating-safety):** #1 US App Store in Jul 2025, downloads +525% WoW, 4M users. Then a breach leaked selfies and IDs. Its viral loop was gossip about a shared target, and trust collapsed.
- **Timeleft (dinners with strangers):** 278-300 cities in ~60 countries. 44K+ dinners and 236K+ people matched. €12.99 per dinner [press]. In-person outcome as the product.
- **BeReal:** daily users fell from ~15M (Oct 2022) to under 6M (Mar 2023). Sold to Voodoo for ~€500M (Jun 2024). Downloads 31.5M (2023) to 12.7M (2024) [3P]. The daily notification was a loop but not a reason; the photos had no lasting value.
- **Gas (Nikita Bier, 2022):** school-by-school rollout timed to school start dates. Sold to Discord. Same tbh playbook. Shows that an atomic school network ignites fast but anonymous compliment polls decay fast.
- **Noplace (2024):** briefly #1 by letting users rank their top-10 friends (MySpace nostalgia). No durable data.

## 2. Patterns that recur across the winners

1. **The invite IS the product, and the receiver needs no app.** Partiful's RSVP link works on the web, and the guest list is the social proof. It reached +400% YoY MAU (500K Q1 2025) and added 2M users in four months with no paid acquisition. Saturn's schedule is the invite ("who's in my lunch?") and it reached ~80% of US high schools. Counter-example: Lapse forced invites, peaked at 218K downloads a day, then fell 70% in a month. Pull-invites compound; forced invites spike and fade.

2. **Win one atomic network at a time, and choose a unit with a hard boundary.** Saturn worked high school by high school (~80% of US HS). Fizz worked campus by campus (620+ campuses, 250 in one month, DAU 4x). Gas went state by state on school start dates. Strava clubs reached 1M (~4x in 2025). Beli started NYC-first, with 80% of growth from referrals.

3. **Content of YOU, or about your people, beats content from strangers.** Locket shows your friend's face on your home screen (9M DAU, DAU/MAU 51%). Strava shows your effort (14B kudos in 2025). Retro shows your week (1.7M MAU, IAP +460%). Yope reports 50%+ of users opening it 5+ days a week. Sora's cameos were also "you," but in a public, novelty frame, and the app died in 6 months. "You" works only inside a close-friend graph.

4. **Taste as identity: the profile is a portfolio of judgments.** Letterboxd grew from 1.8M to 30M+ members, with reviews +49% in 2025. Beli logged 30M reviews in 2024 against Yelp's 21M. Pinterest is at 631M MAU with more than 50% Gen Z. Hinge's prompts-over-swipes gave it +26% revenue against Tinder's -4%. Ranking, logging and curating create a self-portrait that compounds, so switching costs rise with every entry.

5. **Make the input effortless and comparative, not evaluative.** Beli's pairwise "better or worse?" replaces stars and produced 120M ratings. Letterboxd needs only one tap to log. Retro is a weekly camera-roll dump. Locket is a single snap. The lowest-friction atomic action wins participation. Sharp explicitly optimizes for participation rate.

6. **Small, private, no algorithm, no ads (the anti-Instagram).** Locket caps you at 20 friends and is profitable. Retro has no ads and runs on a $36/yr subscription. Yope is private by default, with 15M registered users. Gen Z already ran private "spam" accounts (30%, per Yope's research). The money comes from subscriptions paid by the most engaged few: Locket has 100K+ subscribers, and Retro's spend is up 460%.

7. **Live on the home screen, not inside the app.** Locket (9M DAU), Airbuds (1.5M DAU of 5M MAU, a 30% ratio), Yope's lock-screen widgets, and Locket Rollcall via Live Activity (25%+ of actives post weekly) all use this. The widget turns passive glances into a daily habit without a notification.

8. **Online to offline: the app produces a real-world outcome.** Partiful hosts parties. Strava clubs are 3.5x, and 1 in 5 respondents dated someone from a run club. Timeleft runs 44K dinners. Hinge has 1.9M payers at about 2x Tinder's RPP. Saturn plans your day. Gen Z is paying for apps that get them off apps, and the outcome is the retention mechanism.

9. **Utility first, social second (single-player mode).** Saturn is a schedule before it is a network. Beli is a personal restaurant list before friends join. Strava is a GPS log before kudos. Letterboxd is a diary. Partiful is a free RSVP tool for one host. Each is useful with zero friends, which kills the cold-start problem.

10. **Spikes driven by events, novelty or paid promotion do not retain.** Bluesky has 46M registered but mobile MAU down 52% from peak. Sora fell from 3.3M downloads a month to shutdown in 6 months. Lemon8's US DAU nearly halved within weeks [3P web panel]. BeReal fell from 15M to under 6M DAU. Lapse fell 70%. Tea hit #1, then breached. The durable winners (Letterboxd, Strava, Pinterest, Locket) compounded over 4+ years. Downloads are not the metric; DAU/MAU and weekly participation are (Locket's DAU/MAU is 51%; Bluesky's ~29%).

Related rule of thumb: **monetize the most engaged few through subscriptions or the outcome, not ads**. Examples are Hinge RPP of $33, Strava's ~$500M with ~2% paying, Substack's 5M paid subscriptions at a $1.1B valuation, and Locket profitable on $12.5M raised.
