# Instagram: every project becomes a post

Where the users already are. The account exists to make the feed visible outside the
app and to make posting on Melange feel like getting featured.

- Handle: @melange.app or @melange.collabs (whichever is free; check before claiming).
- Bio: "Find your next collaboration. Photographers, models, musicians, filmmakers.
  A chat only opens when you both say yes." + app link.
- The day a project goes up in the app, it goes up on Instagram: the project's first
  image, title, roles wanted, city, "swipe on it in Melange" in the caption, poster
  tagged. Ask permission in the same message that thanks them for posting.
- When a collab actually happens: a before/after or BTS post, both people tagged.
  This is the only marketing that compounds.
- Stories: one a day during launch week, raw, from Raunak's phone. Who posted, who
  matched, what got made.
- No paid ads until there are ten real collabs to show.

Raunak owns the account and posts. Captions can be drafted here as projects come in.
