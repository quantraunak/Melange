# The ask

Text or Instagram DM, one person at a time, never a group blast. The ask is "post the
thing you're making," not "try my app." Keep it under 60 words.

---

**Someone you know well:**

yo [name], I built an app for creative people to find collaborators (swipe on
projects, chat only opens if you both say yes). Launching it with people I actually
know first. What are you working on right now? Post it and I'll send you the first
people who match. Takes 2 min: [link]

---

**Someone you half know (same school, same team, mutual friend):**

hey [name], Raunak from [SCU tennis / USC CS / Chapman]. I'm launching Melange, an app
where photographers, models, musicians and filmmakers find collaborators and nobody
cold-DMs you. Seeding it with 20 people whose work I like. Would you post the project
you're on right now? [link]

---

**Follow-up, 3 days later, once:**

no pressure, but if you post it this week I'll personally find you a [model /
photographer / whatever they need] for it.

---

**After they post:**

posted, thank you. I'm sending it to [N] people now. Tell me if anyone who matches is
a dud and I'll fix the ranking.
